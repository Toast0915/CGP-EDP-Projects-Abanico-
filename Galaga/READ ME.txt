=========================================================
NEBULA STRIKE
=========================================================

Game Genre:
Arcade Space Shooter / Galaga Inspired Game

Created Using:
Python
Pygame
Tkinter

=========================================================
GAME DESCRIPTION
=========================================================

Nebula Strike is a fast-paced arcade shooter inspired by
classic Galaga-style gameplay. Players control advanced
space fighters and battle waves of alien ships across
multiple levels before facing a giant alien mothership.

The game features smooth enemy formations, unique ship
types, power-ups, boss battles, explosions, animated
effects, and scalable difficulty progression.

=========================================================
GAME FEATURES
=========================================================

[✔] Classic Galaga-Inspired Gameplay

[✔] Smooth Enemy Formation Entry
Enemies fly in from:
- Left side
- Right side
- Top side

[✔] Enemy Formation Movement
After entering formation:
- Enemies move left and right together
- Movement becomes faster on higher levels

[✔] Multiple Levels
- Levels increase in difficulty
- Enemy fire rate increases gradually
- Enemy movement becomes faster

[✔] Boss Battle System
Every few levels:
- Giant alien mothership appears
- Boss has large health bar
- Multiple attack patterns
- Balanced boss difficulty

[✔] Boss Attack Patterns
The boss can:
- Fire triple shots
- Fire spread waves
- Fire rapid random bullets

[✔] 3 Playable Ships

BLUE SHIP
- Balanced fighter
- Medium fire rate
- Medium damage

GREEN SHIP
- Dual rapid-fire ship
- Fast attack speed
- Lower damage
- Good crowd control

PURPLE SHIP
- Heavy laser fighter
- Slow attack speed
- Very high damage
- Best for boss fights

[✔] Power-Up System

Possible Power-Ups:
- Rapid Fire
- Spread Shot
- Extra Life
- Laser Beam

[✔] Laser Beam Power-Up
- Fires a continuous laser
- Damages enemies instantly
- Strong against bosses

[✔] Explosion Effects
- Enemy explosions
- Boss explosions
- Player hit effects

[✔] Damage Indicators
- Floating damage numbers
- Displays damage dealt to enemies and boss

[✔] Animated Background
- Moving starfield effect

[✔] Pause System
Press:
P = Pause / Resume

[✔] Restart System
Press:
R = Restart after Game Over

[✔] Ending Cutscene
After defeating the final boss:
- Mission complete screen
- Animated ship escape
- Back To Menu button

=========================================================
CONTROLS
=========================================================

LEFT ARROW   = Move Left
RIGHT ARROW  = Move Right
SPACEBAR     = Shoot
P            = Pause / Resume
R            = Restart

GAME OBJECTIVE
=========================================================
